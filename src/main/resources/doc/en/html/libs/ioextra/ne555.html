NE555

Library: Input/Output-Extra

Behavior

Models the classic NE555 timer integrated circuit. Supports two operational modes: Astable and Monostable. Timing is simulated in real time coupled with the simulator engine.

Pins

OUT
Digital 1-bit output delivering generated pulses.
TRIG
Active-low trigger input.

Attributes

Mode
Operational mode: Astable or Monostable.
Resistor R1 (1 kΩ to 10 MΩ)
Timing resistor R1. Choose a typical value or type one. A prefixed value is an absolute quantity, as in 10k, 71.66 kΩ, or 1M. Prefix letters are case-sensitive: M is mega and m is milli. The words ohm and ohms are accepted in any case. Spaces are ignored. A bare number is ohms, which is also the unit saved in the circuit file. The value has to land on a whole ohm, so 71.66k is 71660 Ω.
Capacitor C1 (10 µF to 1000 µF)
Timing capacitor C1. A prefixed value is an absolute quantity, as in 10u or 10 µF. The farad symbol is a capital F, and the words farad and farads are accepted in any case. A bare number is microfarads. The value has to land on a whole microfarad, so 10.5µF is rejected.

Timing Formulas

  • Astable: T (ms) = 0.693 × (1000 + 2 × R1[Ohm]) × C1[µF] / 1000
  • Monostable: T (ms) = 1.1 × R1[Ohm] × C1[µF] / 1000

Frequency & Timing Ranges

  • Astable: oscillation frequency up to 48 Hz (period from 21 ms)
  • Monostable: pulse width from 11 ms
