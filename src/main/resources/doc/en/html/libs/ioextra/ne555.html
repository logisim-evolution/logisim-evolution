NE555

Library: Input/Output-Extra

Behavior

Models the classic NE555 timer integrated circuit. Supports two operational modes: Astable and Monostable. Timing is simulated in real time coupled with the simulator engine.

Pins

OUT
Digital 1-bit output delivering generated pulses.
TRIG
Active-low trigger input.

Attributes

Mode
Operational mode: Astable or Monostable.
Resistor R1 (Ohm)
Timing resistor R1 value (1 kΩ to 10 MΩ).
Capacitor C1 (µF)
Timing capacitor C1 value (10 µF to 1000 µF).

Timing Formulas

  • Astable: T (ms) = 0.693 × (1000 + 2 × R1[Ohm]) × C1[µF] / 1000
  • Monostable: T (ms) = 1.1 × R1[Ohm] × C1[µF] / 1000

Frequency & Timing Ranges

  • Astable: oscillation frequency up to 48 Hz (period from 21 ms)
  • Monostable: pulse width from 11 ms
