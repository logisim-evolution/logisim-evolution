74123

The 74123 is a digital model of a dual retriggerable monostable. It follows the datasheet function table, but it does not simulate the analog capacitor. The external timing pins are attributes, not wires. There is no HDL model, and the retrigger recovery time is not modeled.

At 5 V the pulse width is tW = 0.45 × Rext(kΩ) × Cext(pF) nanoseconds, the datasheet formula for a capacitor above 10 nF. That width is remembered when the pulse starts and then elapses in real time while the simulation is running. It does not depend on the tick frequency, so the rest of the circuit can tick slowly or by hand. Stopping the simulation freezes the time that remains, and running again continues it. A later change of R or C applies on the next trigger. A pulse shorter than about 1 ms ends on the next wake-up, which is about 1 ms.

Choose a typical value from the drop-down, or type one. A prefixed value is an absolute quantity: 10k, 1M, 100n, 1 u, 1µF, or 1.0e-6. Prefix letters are case-sensitive: M means mega and m means milli. The farad symbol is a capital F, so 1fF is one femtofarad and 1ff is not. The words ohm, ohms, farad, and farads are also accepted in any case, as in 10 kohm or 100 nfarad. Spaces are ignored. A bare number keeps the saved-file unit: kiloohms or picofarads. The accepted ranges are 2 kΩ to 1 MΩ and 10 nF to 1000 µF.

With 10 kΩ and 100 nF the pulse is about 450 µs. With 1 MΩ and 6.67 µF it is about 3 s.
