TTL library

The TTL library contains digital functional models of 74-series integrated circuits in dual in-line packages. Select a model by its device number. The descriptions below match the names shown in the component catalog.

By default, the power pins are hidden and treated as correctly powered. Enable the Enable Vcc and Gnd ports attribute to expose them. The Show the internal structure attribute displays the internal logic for components that provide that view.

These components model digital logic, not electrical characteristics or physical timing. Refer to the manufacturer's data sheet for the exact pinout, truth table, operating limits, and other device-specific details.

74123 pulse width

The 74123 is a digital model of a dual retriggerable monostable. It follows the datasheet function table, but it does not simulate the analog capacitor. The external timing pins are attributes, not wires. There is no HDL model, and the retrigger recovery time is not modeled.

At 5 V the pulse width is tW = 0.45 × Rext(kΩ) × Cext(pF) nanoseconds, the datasheet formula for a capacitor above 10 nF. That width is converted to simulator ticks at the circuit tick frequency and is remembered when the pulse starts. A later change of R or C applies on the next trigger. If the tick frequency is low, a short pulse lasts one tick: at 1 Hz that is one second. Raise the tick frequency to observe a shorter pulse.

Choose a typical value from the drop-down, or type one. A prefixed value is an absolute quantity, as in SPICE: 10k, 1M, 100n, 1u, 1µF, or 1.0e-6. M means mega and m means milli. A bare number keeps the saved-file unit: kiloohms or picofarads. The accepted ranges are 2 kΩ to 1 MΩ and 10 nF to 1000 µF.

With 10 kΩ and 100 nF the pulse is about 450 µs. With 1 MΩ and 6.67 µF it is about 3 s, which at a 1 Hz tick frequency is three ticks.
